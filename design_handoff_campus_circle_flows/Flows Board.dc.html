This board compares options for five screens. The chat chrome (header, bubbles, composer, blue send button) comes from src/styles.css. The AI surfaces (sheets, source viewer, cards, pinned request) use Industry: square hairline frames, corner marks, Barlow type, steel accent. Content comes from fixtures.ts. The copy here is in English. The prototype will add the 中文 / EN toggle.
Decisions I made for skipped questions: the source viewer defaults to a stacked sheet (alternatives in 1b and 1c). Reviewer mode is a header switch labeled "simulated". Simulated replies are shown, with a label. Ongoing chats and Group knowledge are reached through tabs under the group header.
